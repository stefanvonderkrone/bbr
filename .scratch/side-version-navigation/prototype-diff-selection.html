Help and configuration
  
h or ← selects old. l or → selects new.

  
j or ↓ moves down. k or ↑ moves up.

  
v toggles selection. Shift ↑ and Shift ↓ extend selection. Esc clears selection.

  
y captures exactly the source text in the preview. Wrapped Lines contribute their full text once.

  
Changing Selected Version clears selection. Selecting the current version keeps selection.

  
A version switch keeps a paired row when possible. Otherwise, it chooses the closest source Line in the File.

  
Equal distances choose the following Line. Vertical navigation skips missing Lines in its requested direction.

  
File headers, Hunk headers, Folds, and Comments remain navigation stops. This prototype does not copy those rows.

  
The Sidebar keeps its collapse and expand Actions. These entries apply to the DiffPane.

select_old_version = ["h", "left"]
